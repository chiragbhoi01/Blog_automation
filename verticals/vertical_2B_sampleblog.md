Loom Pricing: Plans, Costs, and Whether It's Worth It
Loom is one of the most recognizable screen recording tools on the market, but its pricing can be confusing once you look past the homepage. Between user seat definitions, limits on the free tier, and an extra cost for AI features, teams often struggle to work out what they will actually end up paying.
The core question is simple: which plan gives you what you need without paying for extras you will not use?
This guide breaks down Loom's current pricing tiers, explains the limits that trigger upgrades, and helps you decide whether Loom fits your workflow.

Loom Pricing Plans
1. Starter (Free)
Price: $0 forever.
Key features: Screen and webcam recording, audio transcription in over 50 languages, viewer comments, emoji reactions, and shared team workspace libraries.
Important limits: Capped at 25 recordings per creator, a strict 5-minute limit per recording, 720p maximum video quality, and no custom branding.
Who it is best for: Individuals and small internal teams testing basic asynchronous video for quick status updates or brief Slack messages.
2. Business
Price: $18 per user per month billed monthly, or 15peruserpermonthbilledannually(180 per user per year).
Key features: Unlimited videos, unlimited recording duration, up to 4K resolution, removal of Loom branding, password-protected videos, video downloads, viewer engagement analytics, and basic waveform trimming.
Important limits: AI features are completely excluded from this tier.
Who it is best for: Teams that record longer product updates, internal documentation, or training videos and do not need automatic AI editing.
3. Business + AI
Price: $24 per user per month billed monthly, or 20peruserpermonthbilledannually(240 per user per year).
Key features: Everything in the Business plan, plus Loom's complete AI suite: automatic removal of filler words ("ums" and "ahs") and silent pauses, auto-generated video titles, summaries, and chapters, transcript-based video editing, and automated meeting notes.
Important limits: The AI package is applied across your workspace user seats rather than purchased as an ad-hoc add-on for a single user.
Who it is best for: Frequent video communicators, managers sharing detailed team recaps, and teams looking to turn spoken videos into written summaries without manual effort.
4. Enterprise
Price: Custom quote (typically negotiated annually based on seat volume).
Key features: Everything in Business + AI, plus Single Sign-On (SAML SSO), automated SCIM user provisioning, compatibility with Atlassian Guard, custom data retention policies, audit logging, Salesforce integration, and dedicated account support.
Important limits: Requires annual contract commitments and formal procurement review.
Who it is best for: Mid-market and enterprise organizations with strict corporate security, compliance, and user governance requirements.
What Do You Actually Get With Each Loom Plan?
Understanding the differences between Loom plans comes down to a few core operational boundaries.
Recording Length and Video Storage
The jump from Starter to Business removes both the 5-minute recording cap and the 25-video ceiling. On Starter, hitting 25 videos forces you to delete older recordings to create new ones. Business removes these limits entirely, making it practical for standard team documentation.
The AI Feature Split
The standard Business plan does not include Loom's AI features. Capabilities like automatic title generation, summaries, transcript-based trimming, and filler-word removal require upgrading to Business + AI. That adds $6 per user per month on monthly billing (or $5 on annual billing). For a 10-person workspace, choosing the AI tier increases your software cost from $1,800 to $2,400 per year.
Viewer Access vs Creator Seats
External clients and internal team members who only need to watch, comment, or react to videos do not require paid creator seats. However, anyone in your organization who needs to record videos longer than 5 minutes or manage workspace content must be assigned a paid seat.
Editing and Quality Controls
Starter caps video output at 720p resolution and basic trimming. The Business tier upgrades video quality up to 4K, unlocks video downloads for local editing, and lets you add clickable call-to-action buttons directly on the video player page.
Is Loom's Free Plan Enough?
Loom's Starter plan is genuinely free with no expiration date, but it is intentionally built for short, light usage.
If your primary goal is to send a sixty-second bug clarification to a coworker or record a two-minute design comment in Slack, the free plan works well.
The free tier stops being practical the moment you need to explain complex workflows. A 5-minute cap makes it difficult to conduct comprehensive software walkthroughs, client project handovers, or full technical demonstrations without splitting videos into multiple parts. Additionally, the 25-video lifetime limit per member means active creators will run out of storage space within their first few weeks.
Which Loom Plan Should You Choose?
Choose Starter ($0) if you only send occasional, short video messages under 5 minutes to coworkers and do not need custom branding.
Choose Business ($15 to $18/seat/month) if your team needs unconstrained recording time, 4K resolution, video downloads, and professional brand controls, but does not rely on automated AI editing.
Choose Business + AI ($20 to $24/seat/month) if you record long-form presentations or meetings regularly and want automatic chapters, written action items, and text-based trimming to save time.
Choose Enterprise (Custom) if your organization requires SAML SSO, centralized identity management, custom data retention schedules, or Atlassian Guard security policies.
Is Loom Worth It?
Whether Loom provides good return on investment depends on your team structure and how your recipients use the videos.
For internal team communication, Loom is usually worth the investment. Replacing low-value status meetings with quick video clips saves time. Loom integrates directly with Slack, Jira, Notion, and Google Workspace, making it easy to drop video messages into existing channels.
For larger internal teams, seat costs can grow quickly. Because Loom charges per creator, licensing a 30-person team on Business + AI costs over $7,000 per year on annual billing. Workspaces must actively manage user roles to ensure occasional recorders do not consume expensive paid seats.
For client handovers and technical documentation, flat video has limitations. Loom outputs a passive video file. When you deliver a 15-minute video to an external client, they often struggle to find specific settings on a flat timeline and may email your team with questions anyway. Loom indexes spoken audio transcripts, but it cannot index silent on-screen clicks, typed inputs, or interface settings that were not spoken aloud.
Loom Pricing vs Alternatives
When evaluating Loom's pricing, it helps to understand why teams look at alternative tools:
Written process documentation: If your team needs click-by-click standard operating procedures rather than full videos, tools like Scribe convert screen actions into screenshot-based guides that are easier to read and maintain.
Developer QA and bug tracking: If your primary focus is reporting software bugs to engineers, specialized extensions like Jam.dev capture browser console logs, network requests, and device specs alongside screen video.
Interactive client handovers and browser walkthroughs: If you deliver custom software or websites to external clients, platforms like Demoly provide browser-based walkthroughs with built-in AI search. Instead of scrubbing a flat timeline, clients can type questions into an AI search box to jump directly to exact visual actions, while sensitive client data is permanently removed before sharing.
Frequently Asked Questions
Is Loom completely free to use?
Yes, the Starter plan is free forever. It allows up to 25 videos per creator with a maximum length of 5 minutes per recording.
What is the price difference between Loom Business and Business + AI?
Business costs 18peruserpermonth(15 billed annually), while Business + AI costs 24peruserpermonth(20 billed annually). The $6 monthly difference covers Loom's automated AI titles, summaries, chapters, and editing tools.
Do external viewers need a paid Loom account to watch videos?
No, external recipients can watch, comment, and react to shared Loom videos in any modern web browser for free without a paid account.
Can you buy Loom AI for just one user on a team plan?
No, Loom applies the AI tier across all creator seats in your workspace, meaning you cannot upgrade a single user while keeping the rest on the standard Business tier.
What happens when you reach the 25-video limit on the free plan?
Once you hit 25 recordings on the Starter plan, you must either upgrade to a paid plan or delete older recordings to make room for new ones.
